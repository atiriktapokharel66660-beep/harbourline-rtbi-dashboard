# KPI Dictionary

Agreed definitions for the 25 KPIs shown in the dashboards (deliverable D2). Changes to a definition need an
approved change request.

| # | KPI | Definition | Dashboard | Refresh |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gross sales | Sum of line price × quantity before discounts and returns | Executive, Store | 5 min |
| 2 | Discounts | Sum of promotional and markdown discounts | Executive, Store | 5 min |
| 3 | Returns | Value of returned items | Executive, Store | 5 min |
| 4 | Net sales | Gross sales − discounts − returns (excl. GST) | Executive, Store | 5 min |
| 5 | Gross margin $ | Net sales − cost of goods sold | Executive | 15 min |
| 6 | Gross margin % | Gross margin $ ÷ net sales | Executive | 15 min |
| 7 | Transactions | Count of completed POS and online orders | Executive, Store | 5 min |
| 8 | Average transaction value | Net sales ÷ transactions | Executive, Store | 5 min |
| 9 | Units per transaction | Units sold ÷ transactions | Store | 5 min |
| 10 | Sales vs target | Net sales ÷ daily store target | Executive, Store | 5 min |
| 11 | Sales vs same day last year | Net sales ÷ net sales on the matching trading day last year − 1 | Executive | 15 min |
| 12 | Online share of sales | Online net sales ÷ total net sales | Executive | 5 min |
| 13 | Cart abandonment rate | Abandoned carts ÷ carts created | Executive | 15 min |
| 14 | Stock on hand | Units available per product and location | Inventory, Store | 5 min |
| 15 | Days of cover | Stock on hand ÷ average daily units sold (last 28 days) | Inventory | 15 min |
| 16 | Stock-out count | Top-500 products with zero stock on hand by location | Inventory, Store | 5 min |
| 17 | Below reorder point | Products whose stock is below the reorder point (triggers alert) | Inventory | 5 min |
| 18 | Overstock count | Products with more than 90 days of cover | Inventory | Daily |
| 19 | Sell-through rate | Units sold ÷ (units sold + stock on hand) for the period | Inventory | 15 min |
| 20 | Open purchase orders | Value of purchase orders not yet received | Inventory | 15 min |
| 21 | Supplier lead time | Average days from PO raised to goods received (last 90 days) | Inventory | Daily |
| 22 | Loyalty sales share | Net sales from loyalty members ÷ total net sales | Executive | 15 min |
| 23 | Active loyalty members | Members with a purchase in the last 90 days | Executive | Daily |
| 24 | Sales per labour hour | Net sales ÷ rostered staff hours | Store | 15 min |
| 25 | Data freshness | Minutes since the latest transaction was loaded | All | 1 min |
